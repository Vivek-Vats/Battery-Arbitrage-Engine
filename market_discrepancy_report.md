# Market Discrepancy Report

## Executive Summary
This report documents the 7 mathematical, regulatory, and financial discrepancies identified between the Battery Arbitrage Engine codebase (`quant_engine.py` & `finance_engine.py`) and European electricity market regulations (ENTSO-E, TenneT NL, PICASSO, EU Directive 2019/944, and EBGL Article 52). All findings were verified through a multi-agent debate between the Grid Rules Expert, Quantitative Analyst, and Financial Auditor, reaching **Unanimous Consensus**.

## Discrepancies & Verified Fixes

| Current Code | ENTSO-E Reality | Proposed PyPSA Fix |
|--------------|-----------------|--------------------|
| `quant_engine.py:156-160`: Inverter constraints `max_p_out` and `max_p_in` sum active DA/Imbalance dispatch, FCR reserve, aFRR reserve, AND real-time aFRR activation power (`p_afrr_up_act`, `p_afrr_down_act`). | Under ENTSO-E SO GL and TenneT PICASSO rules, real-time aFRR activation is the physical execution of committed reserve capacity. The reserve capacity already holds inverter headroom for activation; summing both double-counts inverter power demand. | In `quant_engine.py:156-160`, remove `p_afrr_up_act` from `max_p_out` and `p_afrr_down_act` from `max_p_in` constraints: <br>`max_p_out = p_discharge + p_imb_dis_1 + p_imb_dis_2 + p_imb_dis_3 + fcr_res + afrr_up_1 + afrr_up_2 + afrr_up_3 <= power_mw`<br>`max_p_in = p_charge + p_imb_chg_1 + p_imb_chg_2 + p_imb_chg_3 + fcr_res + afrr_down_1 + afrr_down_2 + afrr_down_3 <= power_mw` |
| `quant_engine.py:169-170`: Reserve energy constraints `Reserve_Energy_Footroom` and `Reserve_Energy_Headroom` evaluate 0.25h AC power reserves directly against battery DC Store SOC (`soc`) without conversion efficiencies. | Under ENTSO-E LER Article 156 (15-min continuous full activation requirement), AC grid delivery of P_AC MW requires drawing (P_AC * 0.25) / eff_dispatch MWh of DC energy from the battery store. Conversely, absorbing P_AC MW AC adds (P_AC * 0.25) * eff_store MWh to the DC store. | Update `quant_engine.py:169-170` reserve energy constraints to incorporate round-trip efficiencies: <br>`m.add_constraints(soc - ((fcr_res + afrr_up_1 + afrr_up_2 + afrr_up_3) * 0.25 / eff_dispatch) >= e_min, name="Reserve_Energy_Footroom")`<br>`m.add_constraints(soc + ((fcr_res + afrr_down_1 + afrr_down_2 + afrr_down_3) * 0.25 * eff_store) <= energy_mwh, name="Reserve_Energy_Headroom")` |
| `quant_engine.py:128` sets `aFRR_Down_Activation` link `marginal_cost = grid_fee_import + afrr_down_act_reward`. `finance_engine.py:82` multiplies activation revenue by `-1` and deducts `grid_fee_import`. | Under EU Directive 2019/944 (Clean Energy Package), German EnWG § 118(6), and TenneT tariff codes, energy storage providing TSO balancing services is exempt from import grid tariffs. Downward activation is a grid service where the battery is paid by the TSO to absorb excess power. | In `quant_engine.py:128`, set `marginal_cost = - afrr_down_act_reward` for `aFRR_Down_Activation`. In `finance_engine.py:82-83`, set `afrr_down_activation_revenue_series = (dispatch_df.get('aFRR_Down_Activation', zero_series) * dispatch_df.get('afrr_down_activation_price_mwh', zero_series)) * time_step_hours` without grid fee deduction, set `grid_fee_import = 0.0` as default for wholesale storage, and include in `total_gross_revenue`. |
| `finance_engine.py:90-143`: Moving Average Tank Ledger (`tank_soc`) accumulates incoming charging volume unconditionally (`new_tank_soc = tank_soc + total_in_vol`) without physical capacity bounds (growing to thousands of MWh). | Physical battery storage inventory cannot exceed nameplate energy capacity `energy_mwh`. Unbounded accumulation dilutes moving average cost (VWAP COGS) with historical memory, locking COGS to unphysical static values. | In `finance_engine.py:126-129`, update tank ledger accumulation to clamp inventory at physical battery capacity: <br>`tank_cost_per_mwh = ((tank_soc * tank_cost_per_mwh) + total_in_cost) / (tank_soc + total_in_vol)`<br>`tank_soc = min(energy_mwh, tank_soc + total_in_vol)` |
| `quant_engine.py:72-74`: `tier2_pu` logic allows `tier1_pu + tier2_pu` to exceed 1.0 p.u. `finance_engine.py:32-64`: applies asset-level tiered price haircuts (0.60/0.10, 0.95/0.50), haircutting Tier 3 revenue by up to 90%. | Under TenneT NL and ENTSO-E EBGL Article 52, balancing capacity auctions settle at a uniform pay-as-cleared price for all awarded MW, and imbalance energy settles at the uniform single 15-minute imbalance price published by the TSO. TSOs do not apply asset-level tiered price haircuts. | In `quant_engine.py:72-74`, strictly partition capacity space: `tier2_pu = ((sat_vol - safe_vol) / power_mw).clip(lower=0.0, upper=1.0 - tier1_pu).fillna(0.0)`. In `finance_engine.py`, settle awarded capacity at full contract price and imbalance energy at single uniform 15-min price `imb_price`. |
| `finance_engine.py:166-174`: Levelized Cost of Storage (`lcos`) omits lifetime electricity charging costs from `total_discounted_lifetime_costs` and sets `annual_delivered_mwh` directly to raw backtest volume `total_vol` without `annualization_factor`. | Standard Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS) methodology requires dividing total discounted lifetime costs (CAPEX + OPEX + lifetime electricity charging costs) by total discounted lifetime delivered MWh, with short-term backtest throughput scaled by `annualization_factor`. | In `finance_engine.py:166-183`, annualize delivered volume: `annual_delivered_mwh = total_vol * annualization_factor`. Calculate discounted lifetime charging cost: `discounted_charging_cost = sum((total_charging_cost * annualization_factor) / ((1 + wacc_decimal) ** t) for t in range(1, int(lifespan_years) + 1))`. Update total costs: `total_discounted_lifetime_costs = TOTAL_CAPEX + discounted_opex + discounted_charging_cost`. |
| `quant_engine.py:182`: `aFRR_Up_Activation` link constraint sets `p_afrr_up_act == (afrr_up_1 + afrr_up_2 + afrr_up_3) * ar_xr`. | In PyPSA, link variable `p0` represents DC power entering the link from `BESS_DC_Bus`. AC power delivered to the grid at `bus1` is p0 * eff_dispatch. Setting p0 = aFRR_up * ar under-delivers AC activation power to the TSO by a factor of eff_dispatch. | In `quant_engine.py:182-183`, scale activation link flow constraint by discharge efficiency: <br>`m.add_constraints(p_afrr_up_act == (afrr_up_1 + afrr_up_2 + afrr_up_3) * ar_xr / eff_dispatch, name="aFRR_Up_Activation_Ratio")`<br>`m.add_constraints(p_afrr_down_act == (afrr_down_1 + afrr_down_2 + afrr_down_3) * ar_xr * eff_store, name="aFRR_Down_Activation_Ratio")` |
